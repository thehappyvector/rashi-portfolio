LUMINUS LIFE+ · WITHIN 2021–2025 GLOBANT TENURE

Building the Growth Strategy Behind a $50M Sovereign-Backed Raise

Problem

Luminus had been building real education outcomes in Jordan for over 20 years, $120M in revenue, real employment numbers behind it. Luminus Life+ was the attempt to take that success across the MENA region, a market facing a genuine $200B talent gap by 2030. The ambition and resources was there. What wasn't there yet was a strategy investors outside Jordan could actually trust.

Constraint

Scaling a proven, single country business into a multi country ecosystem required more than replicating the existing plan at a larger size. It needed a growth strategy credible enough to convince investors who'd never operated in these markets that the math actually worked.

The Insight

A model that already worked in one country with physical presence wasn't a smaller version of the regional opportunity. Making the regional opportunity believable relied on delivering courses digitally in environments where trained educators were scarce. The scalability problem was the actual blocker, not the market size.

Approach

I started with the market sizing. I built out a $200B total opportunity, narrowed to a realistic $26B serviceable market, and a $2B obtainable target in MENA by 2030, growing to $8B globally when measured against comparable education businesses.

To solve the scarce-trainer problem directly, I identified existing nonprofits and active community groups already operating in candidate countries, organizations that could deliver the digital courses locally without Luminus needing to build a trainer network from zero. That identification work fed directly into the market sizing and became a real input into the expansion sequencing itself, a hub and spoke model based in Dubai, moving from 2 pilot countries in 2023 to 4 in 2024 to 8 by 2030, prioritized by GDP per capita, internet access, regulatory environment, and the presence of these delivery partners on the ground.

Outcome

Working directly with the founder, I built the pitch and the investment narrative behind this strategy. He's the one who carried it into the room. Luminus Life+ came out of that with a $50M seed investment from Saudi Arabia's Public Investment Fund.
